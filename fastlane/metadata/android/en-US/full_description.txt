Learn a language while you scroll.

Clip Captions shows what people say in TikTok, Reels, Shorts and other videos as text, translated into your language, in a small box over the video. It reads the sound your phone is playing, so it works with AirPods and headphones in.

• Instant: words appear as they're spoken. With more than one language ticked, it works out each video's language for you.
• Any language: works out the language of every video itself.
• Swipe to the next video and the captions clear.
• Tap ☆ to save a phrase, then find it in My phrases to copy, share or learn.
• Saves battery: it pauses when nothing is playing.
• Free, and nothing is recorded or uploaded. Speech recognition and translation run on your phone.

Languages download once, before captions start, so nothing downloads while you're scrolling.

Needs Android 10 or newer. Instant needs Android 13 or newer with Google's on-device speech recognition.

Clip Captions isn't made by, or connected with, TikTok, Instagram or YouTube.
